WHY PET HEALTH

Useful on an ordinary Tuesday.
Indispensable on a difficult one.

01

Write it as it happened.

Keep your original words alongside a clear, structured record. No need to remember the perfect clinical term.

Observations, weights, medication, and lab results stay together instead of scattered across notes and messages.

03

Give your vet a read-only link that expires and can be revoked. Your household stays in control.
